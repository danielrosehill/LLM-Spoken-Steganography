Spoken steganography

An encrypted message hidden in the sentence openers of a dull podcast, spoken by one phone and decoded by another. Background: My Weird Prompts #6141, “Overheard at the Laundrette”.

How it was read

The demo this repo grew out of. A seven-minute laundrette scene between Dorothy and Tim. It hides one sentence in the first letter of each of Dorothy's 23 lines, with no key. Listen first, then reveal.

    Herman, on air: “A serious version would add a key… Encrypt the content, then hide that it exists.” The Send and Receive tabs are that serious version.
